# Institution and award emblems

Retrieved and checked on 2026-10-03. These are third-party institutional marks,
not original artwork covered by this project's MIT license. The SVGs retain
their source geometry, proportions, and colors.

- Official identity reference: [Beihang University — university emblem](https://www.buaa.edu.cn/xygk/jrbh/bhxh.htm).

- Rights: the emblem belongs to Beihang University. The source provides no explicit Creative Commons or other open license for the mark; its [terms](https://cdnlogo.com/terms) reserve copyright and trademark rights. Inclusion here identifies the university and does not imply university endorsement or a grant of unrestricted reuse rights.
- Original viewBox: `0 0 213 213`; original blue is preserved.

## National Emblem of the PRC — `national-scholarship.svg`

- File description and rights statement: [Wikimedia Commons: 中華人民共和國國徽.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E4%B8%AD%E8%8F%AF%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9C%8B%E5%9C%8B%E5%BE%BD.svg).
- Downloaded vector: [original SVG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6b/%E4%B8%AD%E8%8F%AF%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9C%8B%E5%9C%8B%E5%BE%BD.svg).
- Source revision: 30 April 2026; uploader HyBoxwood. The source describes a vector derived from the government-published 3D national-emblem model, following GB 15093-2025, and credits the original emblem's designers, including Lin Huiyin and Liang Sicheng.
- Official reference: [Chinese government national-emblem resources](https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/guohui/). The included SVG is the Commons vector rendition, not a government-hosted SVG download.
- Rights: Commons marks the artwork public domain as material derived from a mandatory Chinese national standard. National-emblem use restrictions are separate from copyright status; this file is not licensed under the site's MIT license.
- The filename records its placement beside a National Scholarship entry. The image is the national emblem, **not a separate National Scholarship logo**.
- Original viewBox: `0 0 1993.95996 2162.68005`; original red, gold, gradients, and internal vector references are preserved.

## Local processing

Only XML serialization, whitespace cleanup, and removal of non-rendering editor
metadata were performed. No paths, coordinates, colors, proportions, or gradient
definitions were redrawn. All three assets were checked for valid SVG/XML and
contain no scripts, event handlers, foreign objects, linked resources, or raster
images. Internal fragment references used by the national emblem's gradients
and clipping paths are retained.
