# Competition and company icons

Retrieved or created on 2026-10-03. These are standalone SVGs with no scripts,
embedded bitmap images, external fonts, or external resource dependencies.
Brand names and marks identify the organizations associated with the listed
experience; their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

## ICPC — `icpc.svg`

- Official press-kit confirmation: [ICPC News Press](https://news.icpc.global/press/) provides [the original SVG](https://news.icpc.global/icpc_foundation.svg). Its contents match the original SVG from the official ICPC GitHub repository below.
- All three ICPC award entries use this compact official mark. Gold and Silver remain in the award titles; no generic medal is overlaid on the logo.

- Source: the official ICPC GitHub organization, repository
  [`icpc/ewpc-web`](https://github.com/icpc/ewpc-web), which maintains the
  European Women's Programming Contest website.
- Original vector:
  [`assets/icpc_foundation.svg` at commit `c1c6e2c9b3dd81615e71720b03b40c7ef14fd8fa`](https://github.com/icpc/ewpc-web/blob/c1c6e2c9b3dd81615e71720b03b40c7ef14fd8fa/assets/icpc_foundation.svg).
- Adaptation: retained the original blue, yellow, and red emblem paths and
  removed only the `icpc.foundation` wordmark paths. The viewBox was tightened
  around the remaining emblem. Original geometry and colors (`#4C82C3`,
  `#FDBA11`, `#B22D1B`) are unchanged. This is the ICPC Foundation emblem,
  not a redrawn or invented competition logo.
- Rights: ICPC/ICPC Foundation branding. No separate open license for this
  artwork was found in that repository; this source record does not grant
  trademark rights or represent the mark as public domain.

## Ninebot — `ninebot.svg`

- Source page: [Ninebot official support website](https://support.ninebot.com/us-en/).
- Original vector: the `ic-nine-logo` symbol in the site's
  [official SVG sprite](https://support.ninebot.com/_nuxt/icons.BhyOx0Ht.svg).
- Adaptation: extracted the four original contours of the circular symbol
  from the full `ninebot 九号` logo. The wordmark was omitted. Path commands
  were normalized to absolute coordinates without changing the geometry;
  the original black fill is retained. The symbol occupies `0 0 24 24`;
  the standalone viewBox is `-2 -2 28 28` to add optical padding at small
  sizes. The padding adjustment does not alter any path geometry.
- Rights: Ninebot/Segway branding remains owned by the respective rights
  holders. The official [intellectual-property statement](https://www.segway.com/content/experience-fragments/segway-ninebot/us/en/segway/info/terms/intellectual-property/master.html)
  reserves copyright and trademark rights; no open-source license is claimed
  for this brand mark.

## Challenge Cup — `challenge-cup.svg`

- Uses the original [Phosphor Regular trophy SVG](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phosphor-icons/core/main/assets/regular/trophy.svg), with its geometry unchanged. The blue-grey colour adapts to light/dark mode.
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2023 Phosphor Icons. The full text is retained in `Phosphor-LICENSE.txt`. This is a generic award symbol, **not the official Challenge Cup emblem**.
- Official alternatives were checked: the [official SVG wordmark](https://www.tiaozhanbei.net/static/images/mobile/mobile_header_logo.svg) and the [19th competition's purple wordmark](https://2025.tiaozhanbei.net/all_static/2025guosai/tzb2025/images/tzb_logo.png). Removing the small subtitle from the official SVG leaves the three original calligraphic characters in a roughly 121:27 frame. At 32px wide, they are only about 7px high, too small to read clearly beside an entry title.
- The Phosphor trophy was selected after comparing actual small-size renders of Phosphor regular/filled, Tabler outline/filled, and Remix line/filled. It shares the same family as the teaching, presidium, and ranking icons; the previous custom gold trophy has been replaced.
