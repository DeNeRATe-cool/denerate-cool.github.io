Selection notes:
Phosphor regular and duotone, Tabler outline, Remix line, Lucide, and Iconoir
were reviewed. Regular Phosphor was selected for the related teaching/group/
ranking shapes and its restrained line weight at small sizes. The duotone fills,
rosette ornaments, and mixed stroke weights were not used in this set.
